About us

A small, director-led company based in Watford

CCM CONSTRUCT LTD is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 10433659. It was incorporated on 18 October 2016 and is based in Watford, Hertfordshire.

The company provides support services for construction projects and for businesses. That means helping with the organising side of project work: planning, coordination, paperwork and general business support.

The company is run by its director, so you deal directly with the person responsible for the work, without layers of account managers in between.

Services

How we can help

Our work centres on the support that keeps projects and businesses running smoothly. Get in touch to talk through what you need.

Project support

Help with the planning side of project work: setting out the stages, keeping track of what needs to happen and when, and following up so that tasks don't slip through the gaps.

  • Planning and organising project stages
  • Tracking progress against the plan
  • Follow-up on open actions

Coordination

Keeping the different parties on a project working to the same timetable. We help line up schedules, pass information between the people involved and keep everyone up to date.

  • Scheduling and timetabling
  • Liaising with suppliers and other parties
  • Clear, regular updates

Project administration

The paperwork that comes with project work, handled in an orderly way. We help keep documents, records and correspondence organised so the information is there when it is needed.

  • Document and record keeping
  • Correspondence and filing
  • Supplier and order paperwork

Business support

General support for businesses that need an extra pair of hands with the organising and administrative side of things, in line with the business support activities the company is registered for.

  • Administrative support
  • Organising day-to-day tasks
  • Support tailored to what you need
